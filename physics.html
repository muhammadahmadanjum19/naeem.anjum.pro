Radiation Physics and Chemistry (2025)
Chitosan coated Yb-doped MnCo ferrite: tailoring properties via gamma irradiation for contrast agents and sensors
Touseef Rafiq, Bushra Shaheen, Muhammad Hassan, Adeel Ur-Rehman, Muhammad Naeem Anjum
Abstract
This study investigates the systematic modification of Ytterbium (Yb)-doped manganese-cobalt ferrite nanoparticles coated with chitosan. By employing controlled gamma irradiation, significant alterations in structural parameters, magnetic response, and surface-active sites were achieved, demonstrating exceptional suitability for advanced biomedical MRI contrast enhancement and targeted diagnostic sensor applications.

Radiation Physics and Chemistry (2024)
Gamma-ray-elicited alterations in structural, optical, and morphological attributes of spinel nickel cobaltite NiCo2O4
Bushra Shaheen, Nida Shaheen, Muhammad Naeem Anjum
Abstract
The influence of gamma-ray exposure on the structural, optical bandgap, and morphological characteristics of spinel nickel cobaltite (NiCo2O4) nanostructures is examined in detail. The findings reveal defect-induced lattice modifications and tunable optical responses, providing critical insights for radiation-hardened energy storage and sensor devices.
